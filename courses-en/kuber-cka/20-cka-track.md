# 20. CKA track

From here the pages are **tasks**. No `kubectl` recipe. Any correct end state passes.

Use `LABCTL_MODE=cka` when you want exam silence. Stay on `training` if you want `python -m labctl hint` and an explanation after Check.

Work in a **lab namespace** (`cka-01`, …). Do not break `shop` unless a later troubleshooting lab says so.

Cluster must still be healthy: three Ready nodes, CoreDNS, CNI. Start will refuse otherwise.
