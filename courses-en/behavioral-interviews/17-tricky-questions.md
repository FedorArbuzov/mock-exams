# 17. Hard and trick questions

## Intro

"Your weakness?", "Tell me about a conflict with your boss", "Why the gap in your CV" — traps or a maturity check. Not to be improvised.

---

## Greatest weakness

| Bad | Good |
|-------|--------|
| "A perfectionist" (cliché) | A real growth area + what you're doing about it |
| "I work too much" | "Delegation — I'm learning through …" |

Formula: weakness → impact → **active steps** → progress.

---

## Gap / job hopping

Briefly the fact → what you were doing (learning, capstone, family) → readiness to work.

---

## Conflict with manager

Only if there's a **mature** ending; without blame. Otherwise pick a peer conflict ([06](06-conflict.md)).

---

## "Where do you see yourself in 5 years?"

Tie it to the company's trajectory: tech lead / staff / deep IC — honestly and consistent with the role's level.

---

## Illegal / inappropriate

Questions about family, health, religion — politely: "I prefer to discuss relevant experience"; in Russia/EU, know your rights.

---

## Subtasks

**Time:** ~55 min.

### 17.1 Weakness (15 min)

A real weakness + a plan; no clichés.

### 17.2 Gap/hop (15 min)

If applicable — 3 sentences.

### 17.3 5 years (10 min)

An answer tailored to the target company.

### 17.4 Trap list (15 min)

5 scary questions + a draft answer.
