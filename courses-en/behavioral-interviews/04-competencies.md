# 04. Competencies: what they look for by level

## Intro

Companies name competencies differently (Amazon Leadership Principles, Google googliness, "our values"). The essence is the same: **repeatable behaviors**. A competency → story map speeds up your prep.

---

## Universal competencies (engineer)

| Competency | What to prove |
|-------------|--------------|
| **Ownership** | end-to-end, not "not my area" |
| **Bias for action** | a decision with incomplete data |
| **Dive deep** | root cause, not a symptom patch |
| **Deliver results** | outcome on time |
| **Earn trust** | honesty, transparency |
| **Disagree and commit** | argue → decide → support |
| **Learn and be curious** | growth, feedback |
| **Mentor / develop** | helped others grow |

---

## Amazon LP (condensed)

Often show up at international companies and their clones:

| LP | Story from |
|----|------------|
| Customer Obsession | a decision in the user's favor vs internal convenience |
| Ownership | on-call, follow-through after a release |
| Invent and Simplify | removed complexity |
| Insist on Highest Standards | a quality gate in CI |
| Bias for Action | MVP vs analysis paralysis |
| Learn and Be Curious | a new stack / certification with real application |
| Hire and Develop the Best | interviewing, mentoring |
| Frugality | cost-aware design ([finops](../finops/README.md)) |

You don't need to cram all 16 — **6–8** relevant stories cover most questions.

---

## Middle vs Senior

| | Middle | Senior |
|--|--------|--------|
| Scope | task / feature | service / domain / team |
| Influence | team of 3–8 | cross-team |
| Uncertainty | asks the lead | frames the problem themselves |
| Mistakes | admits, fixes | changes the process so it doesn't recur |

---

## Subtasks

**Time:** ~50 min.

### 4.1 JD mapping (20 min)

Take a real job posting; write out 6 competencies from the text → map them to the story bank (have it / don't).

### 4.2 Gap plan (15 min)

3 competencies with no story — where to get them (work, capstone, open source).

### 4.3 LP pick (15 min)

Pick 6 LPs; one line "my story ID" for each.

---

## Summary

Job posting → competencies → card ID. Without a map you're preparing for "every question in the world".
