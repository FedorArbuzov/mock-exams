# Interview cheatsheet — Behavioral

A quick reference before a round. Full course: [README](README.md).

---

## STAR (2–3 min)

| S | T | A | R |
|---|---|---|---|
| context 15% | your role 10% | **your** steps 50% | metric + learning 25% |

---

## Tell me about yourself

**Present → Past (the relevant parts) → Future (this company)**

30 sec and 2 min — both versions ready.

---

## Must-have stories (8)

1. Best contribution (ownership)  
2. Conflict (collaboration)  
3. Failure (growth)  
4. Deadline (prioritization)  
5. Influence without a title (leadership)  
6. Mentoring (teamwork)  
7. Tech trade-off (judgment)  
8. Initiative (initiative)  

---

## Phrases

- "Specifically, **I** did …"
- "Result: … (metric)"
- "If it were now — …"
- "Let me think for a moment" (EN)

---

## Don't do

- Trash talk about an employer  
- Only "we"  
- No failures  
- "No questions"  

---

## Reverse (top 5)

1. On-call for the role?  
2. What changed after the last incident?  
3. First 90 days success?  
4. What does code review look like?  
5. A strong hire at this level?
