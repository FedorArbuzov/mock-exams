# 18. Remote, async, English

## Intro

Distributed teams are a separate behavioral area: communication, timezone, documentation. International companies mean **English** on the same STAR.

---

## Remote / async

| Competency | Story |
|-------------|---------|
| Over-communication | statuses, RFCs in Notion |
| Documentation | ADR instead of "let's hop on a call" |
| Timezone | handoff, overlap windows |
| Trust | deliver without micromanagement |

Link: [devops-culture/10](../devops-culture/10-cicd-culture.md) — async culture.

---

## English behavioral

| Tip | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Simple phrases | better than complex ones with mistakes |
| Same STAR | structure in English is simpler than "pretty" prose |
| Pause | "Let me think for a moment" — is fine |
| Tech terms | API, deployment, on-call — don't translate |

Prepare **2 stories** fully in English (written + out loud).
