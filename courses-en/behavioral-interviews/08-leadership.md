# 08. Leadership and influence without a title

## Intro

"Leadership" in an interview ≠ "was a team lead". It's **shifting the system** without formal authority: an RFC, a standard, a cross-team initiative.

---

## Examples for an engineer

| Situation | Leadership signal |
|----------|-------------------|
| Introduced contract tests in 3 repos | quality standard |
| Organized an observability guild | community |
| Convinced security of a pragmatic rollout | influence |
| Ran a release as release captain | ownership |

Senior: **others repeat** your pattern without your involvement.
