# 15. Your questions for the interviewer

## Intro

"Any questions?" — **yes**, always. Silence = low interest. Questions show your level and help you **evaluate the employer**.

---

## Categories

| Category | Examples |
|-----------|---------|
| Role | first 90 days, success metrics |
| Team | size, on-call, boundaries with platform |
| Tech | stack, legacy, CI/CD maturity |
| Process | releases, code review, postmortem |
| Growth | mentoring, conferences, grades |
| Culture | remote, meeting load |

---

## Strong questions

- "What does on-call look like for this role?"
- "The last serious incident — what did you change afterward?"
- "How do you make the build vs buy decision for the platform?"
- "What sets apart a strong hire at this level?"

---

## Weak / premature

- Salary in the first tech round (usually goes to the recruiter).
- "What does the company do?" (you should know from research).
- Questions answered on the website.

---

## By round

| Round | To whom |
|-------|------|
| Manager | growth, priorities, feedback |
| Peer | day-to-day, review, tech debt |
| Bar raiser | culture, standards |

3–5 questions per round; note down what you've already asked.

---

## Subtasks

**Time:** ~50 min.

### 15.1 A list of 15 (25 min)

5 on the role, 5 on tech, 5 on culture.

### 15.2 Personalization (15 min)

For one real company — the top 5.

### 15.3 No-go zones (10 min)

What you'll only learn after an offer (fine to defer).
