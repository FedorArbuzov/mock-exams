# 16. Panel, bar raiser, culture fit

## Intro

Several interviewers or a "bar raiser" from another team mean **deep follow-ups** and a consistency check of your stories. Contradictions between rounds = red flag.

---

## Panel interview

| Tip | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Eye contact | with each in turn |
| Names | write them down at the start |
| Questions | clarify who the answer is addressed to |

One story — **the same** in facts; a different emphasis is fine.

---

## Bar raiser / culture

External to the team; can veto. Prepare for:

- "Why weren't you promoted at your last job?"
- "The toughest feedback you received?"
- "When did you break a rule for the user's sake?"

Honesty + reflection.

---

## Consistency log

After each round: which stories you told, which numbers you cited — so you don't contradict yourself in the final.

---

## Subtasks

**Time:** ~45 min.

### 16.1 Consistency table (20 min)

Story | metrics | dates (rounded) | whom you told.

### 16.2 Hard feedback (15 min)

STAR: received tough feedback → change.

### 16.3 Panel sim (10 min)

A friend asks 3 questions in a row as different "roles".
