# Behavioral Interviews

A theory course on **behavioral (soft skills) interviews** for engineers: **STAR**, story bank, conflicts, failures, leadership, motivation, reverse questions, and mock scenarios. A "book" format in English with **subtasks**; **no sandbox** — practice happens in the `story-bank.md` document.

**Who it's for:** backend, DevOps, platform, SRE — before **middle+** interviews (product, fintech, Big Tech, strong startup), where after algorithms and design come **1–3 rounds** "about experience".

**Prerequisites:**

| Skill | Why |
|-------|--------|
| 1+ year of commercial experience **or** strong pet/capstone projects | material for stories |
| A completed capstone | [fastapi/42](../fastapi/42-capstone.md), [gitlab-advanced/15](../gitlab-advanced/15-final-project.md) |

**Useful in parallel:** [python-algorithms](../python-algorithms/README.md), [microservices-patterns](../microservices-patterns/README.md), [devops-culture](../devops-culture/README.md), [sre/09](../sre/09-postmortems.md).

## How to read

- Chapters **01–17** — ~**40–55 min** (theory + **subtasks**).
- Chapters **18–20** — mock and **final story portfolio** (**3–4 h**).
- Keep a **`story-bank.md`** from the first chapter — don't put it off until the end.
- Stories **without NDA**: round the metrics, replace the names.

**Time:** ~**12–16 hours**.

## What you should end up with

- **8–12** rehearsed STAR stories across different competencies.
- A "tell me about yourself" answer for **2 and 5 minutes** with no filler.
- An honest story about a **failure** with learning (not a victim story).
- **10+** questions for the employer without ever saying "no questions".
- A **Story Portfolio** file for quick review before a round.
