# 01. Why behavioral and how you're evaluated

## Intro

"I write great code" — but at middle+ they also look at **how you work with people, uncertainty, and failures**. Behavioral is not a psych test; it's a **prediction of your behavior on a team** based on past examples.

---

## What is evaluated

| Signal | The question in the interviewer's head |
|--------|----------------------------|
| **Ownership** | will you see it through without reminders |
| **Collaboration** | can you argue constructively |
| **Growth** | do you learn from mistakes |
| **Communication** | will the PM/junior understand your explanation |
| **Judgment** | priorities when time is scarce |
| **Culture add** | will you strengthen the team, not just "fit" |

---

## Formats

| Format | Duration | Notes |
|--------|--------------|-------------|
| Hiring manager | 45–60 min | motivation, growth, fit |
| Peer / team | 45 min | collaboration, code review |
| Bar raiser / culture | 60 min | high bar, deep follow-up |
| Panel | 2–3 interviewers | different angles on the same competencies |
| Recruiter screen | 20–30 min | short STAR, salary, logistics |

Technical rounds are in [python-algorithms](../python-algorithms/README.md); this chapter comes **after** or **between** them.

---

## What is NOT behavioral

| Question | Type |
|--------|-----|
| "Implement an LRU cache" | coding |
| "Design Uber" | system design |
| "What is the GIL?" | technical Q&A |
| "Tell me about a conflict with a colleague" | **behavioral** |

Mixed rounds: "tell me how you debugged prod" — behavioral **with technical** content ([10-technical-decision](10-technical-decision.md)).

---

## How the interviewer takes notes

Usually: competency → quotes/facts → **hire / no hire / lean**. Your answers must give **verifiable** details: what **you** did, a number, a timeframe.

---

## Red flags for the candidate

- Every story is "we" with no "I".
- Others are always to blame.
- Not a single failure.
- Vague metrics ("it got better").
- Toxicity about past employers.

---

## Subtasks

**Time:** ~45 min.

### 1.1 Round map (15 min)

For your target company (or a typical product) write out: round | format | likely competencies.

### 1.2 Self-audit (15 min)

Rate 1–5: ownership, collaboration, communication, handling failure. The weakest one is the course focus.

### 1.3 A "we" story (10 min)

Take one work win; rewrite 3 sentences with emphasis on **I** + result.

### 1.4 NDA check (5 min)

A list of topics you **can't** name; what to replace them with (generalization, synthetic capstone).

---

## Summary

Behavioral is about **evidence** of behavior, not charisma. Prepare concrete episodes in advance.
