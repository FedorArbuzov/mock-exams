# 12. Why you're leaving, why us, why this role

## Intro

Motivation questions are about **risk**: will you leave in 3 months, will you fail to agree on level, did you come "just to try it out". Prepare the answers **in advance**, without improvising on emotion.

---

## Why leaving (current job)

| Fine | Better to avoid |
|-------|----------------|
| Growth, a new domain, scale | "Toxic CEO" |
| Stagnation, no tech roadmap | "They pay little" (in the first round) |
| Relocation, remote policy | Listing grievances |

Formula: **toward**, not just **away from**.

"I want stronger platform practices and a product metric — here the focus is on outsourcing contracts."

---

## Why us

Research the company: product, a blog post, a talk, open source, the stack from the JD.

| Weak | Strong |
|-------|--------|
| "A big company" | "Your approach to SLOs in blog post X matches my postmortem experience" |
| "An interesting product" | "I use it as a user; I see growth in …" |

---

## Why this role

Connect **your** past to **their** JD bullet by bullet.

---

## Salary / level (recruiter)

Briefly: range, flexibility, focus on fit. Details — after the technical rounds. Don't lowball yourself without a reason.

---

## Subtasks

**Time:** ~60 min.

### 12.1 Why leaving (15 min)

3 sentences; check for toxicity.

### 12.2 Why us (20 min)

5 facts about the company + a link to you.

### 12.3 Why role (15 min)

A table: JD requirement | your experience.

### 12.4 Red team (10 min)

A friend/colleague asks "sounds like a runaway" — your answer.
