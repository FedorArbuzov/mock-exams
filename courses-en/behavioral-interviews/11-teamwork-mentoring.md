# 11. Teamwork and mentoring

## Intro

"I worked on a team" is a trivial answer. You need **specifics**: code review, pair solving, onboarding, splitting up work, unblocking blockers.

---

## Topics

| Question | Angle |
|--------|------|
| A difficult colleague | empathy + boundaries ([06](06-conflict.md)) |
| Helped a junior | mentoring, measurable growth |
| Cross-functional team | PM, QA, design |
| Code review | how you give feedback |

---

## Feedback (SBI)

**Situation – Behavior – Impact** for a review:

"In PR #42 (S) you proposed a rename without tests (B) — a regression risk in billing (I). We added a contract test together."

---

## Subtasks

**Time:** ~55 min.

### 11.1 Mentoring (20 min)

STAR: whom you helped; what changed over 1–3 months.

### 11.2 Code review (20 min)

A story of a constructive, tough review (you gave or received it).

### 11.3 Remote collaboration (15 min)

How you stayed in sync (async doc, standup) — relevant for [18](18-remote-english.md).
