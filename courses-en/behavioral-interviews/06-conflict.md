# 06. Conflict and disagreement

## Intro

"Tell me about a conflict" is a test of **emotional maturity**. They're looking for data, respect, outcome — not "I was right, he was an idiot".

---

## A good conflict story

| Element | Content |
|---------|------------|
| Stakes | what was at stake (deadline, quality, users) |
| Positions | two positions **without** labels |
| Your move | 1:1, facts, prototype, ADR |
| Resolution | compromise or disagree-and-commit |
| Relationship | you keep working together |

**Topics:** PM vs deadline, dev vs dev (architecture), ops vs feature freeze.

---

## Disagree and commit

```text
"I argued for feature flags; the team chose a big bang.
I documented the risks, took on the rollback plan task, and supported the release."
```

Shows maturity more strongly than "I was talked out of it".

---

## Antipatterns

- A conflict with a "toxic" person without your role in the resolution.
- Personal attacks in the retelling.
- A conflict without resolution (left hanging).
- Picking a story about a jerk of a former boss.

---

## Subtasks

**Time:** ~60 min.

### 6.1 Story-bank card (25 min)

Full STAR: a technical or process conflict.

### 6.2 Both sides (10 min)

2 sentences each of "their logic was…" without sarcasm.

### 6.3 Alternative (15 min)

"What would you do differently now?"

### 6.4 Short version (10 min)

60 seconds for a recruiter.
