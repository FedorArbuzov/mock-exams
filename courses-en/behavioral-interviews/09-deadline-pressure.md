# 09. Deadlines, priorities, overload

## Intro

"Tell me about a tough deadline" is about **prioritization**, communicating risks, and quality under pressure — not about "worked 80 hours" (a red flag for many companies).

---

## A good answer

| Step | Action |
|-----|----------|
| Clarify | scope, must-have vs nice-to-have |
| Communicate | an early signal to PM/stakeholders |
| Cut | defer, MVP, feature flags |
| Protect | don't silently burn quality (tests on the critical path) |
| Result | what you delivered, what you deferred |

---

## Work-life boundary

If there was an intense week — fine to mention **briefly**; emphasize the **planning**, not the heroics. Companies with a burnout-aware culture get wary of "I always pull all-nighters".

---

## Subtasks

**Time:** ~55 min.

### 9.1 STAR deadline (25 min)

A real release or incident + tight timelines.

### 9.2 Scope cut list (15 min)

A table: feature | must? | outcome.

### 9.3 "No" (15 min)

A story where you **declined** a task — how you said it and what you replaced it with.
