# 03. Story bank: collecting and revising stories

## Intro

Recalling a "fitting case" from scratch during an interview is stressful. A **story bank** is 8–12 prepared stories tagged with competencies; for a question you **pick** rather than improvise from scratch.

---

## Card template

```markdown
## ID: billing-idempotency
**Competencies:** ownership, technical judgment, collaboration
**Level:** middle+
**S:** ...
**T:** ...
**A:** (3–5 bullets)
**R:** metric + learning
**Follow-ups:** what you'd do differently; how you convinced skeptics
**Don't mention:** the bank's name, exact amounts
```

File: `story-bank.md` in the root of your prep (next to [python-algorithms mistakes](../python-algorithms/01-interview-landscape.md)).

---

## Minimal set (8 stories)

| # | Topic | Competency |
|---|------|-------------|
| 1 | Best technical contribution | ownership |
| 2 | Conflict with a colleague/PM | collaboration |
| 3 | Failure / mistake in prod | growth |
| 4 | Hard deadline | prioritization |
| 5 | Influence without authority | leadership |
| 6 | Mentoring / onboarding | teamwork |
| 7 | Disagreeing with a technical decision | judgment |
| 8 | Initiative beyond your tasks | initiative |

Add 2–4 from a capstone ([fastapi/42](../fastapi/42-capstone.md)) if you have little commercial experience — be honest: "a learning project, but production-like".

---

## One episode — several questions

A postmortem story covers:

- "Tell me about a failure"
- "How do you work under pressure"
- "An example of improving a process"

Don't reuse **one** episode for all 8 slots — interviewers compare variety.

---

## Revision every 2 weeks

| Check | Action |
|----------|----------|
| Outdated? | update the metrics |
| Too long? | trim S/T |
| NDA? | anonymize |
| No "I"? | rewrite the Action |

---

## Subtasks

**Time:** ~60 min.

### 3.1 Create story-bank.md (10 min)

Copy the card template × 8 empty sections.

### 3.2 Fill in 3 cards (30 min)

Full STAR: conflict, failure, best contribution.

### 3.3 Tags (10 min)

A table: interviewer's question → story ID.

### 3.4 Capstone fallback (10 min)

If < 2 years of experience — one card from a mock-exams capstone marked as synthetic.

---

## Summary

A story bank is a **2–3 hour investment** that pays off in every behavioral round.
