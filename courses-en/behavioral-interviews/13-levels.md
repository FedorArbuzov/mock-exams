# 13. Junior / Middle / Senior: different bar

## Intro

The same question is evaluated **differently**. A senior with no cross-team or process — a downgrade; a junior with "I rewrote the company's architecture" — not believed.

---

## Expectations (generalized)

| | Junior | Middle | Senior |
|--|--------|--------|--------|
| Scope | task | feature/service | domain / several teams |
| Autonomy | with support | clarifies on their own | frames the problem |
| Mistakes | learns | fixes + alert | changes the system |
| Leadership | learns from others | mentors juniors | standards, RFC |
| Stories | study + 1–2 jobs | prod impact | scale, org change |

---

## Over-leveling

Don't sell senior stories at middle: a bar raiser will catch the shallow depth.

---

## Under-leveling

A middle with only "closed tickets" is weak. You need **one** end-to-end example.

---

## Subtasks

**Time:** ~45 min.

### 13.1 Target level (10 min)

Which level you're going for; why not ±1.

### 13.2 Rewrite a story (20 min)

One card — a middle version and a senior version (depth).

### 13.3 Gap (15 min)

If you're aiming for senior — 3 gaps and a 6-month plan.
