Why won't your container image download?

That is ErrImagePull, and after a few retries it becomes ImagePullBackOff - Kubernetes backing off before trying again. The container never starts because the kubelet cannot fetch the image. The fix is almost always one of four things.

First, the name or tag is wrong - a typo, or a tag that does not exist in the registry. Second, the registry is private and you have no valid imagePullSecret, so you get an unauthorized error. Third, the node cannot reach the registry - DNS or network egress is blocked. Fourth, you hit a rate limit, classic with anonymous Docker Hub pulls.

What beginners get wrong: staring at logs, which are empty because no container ran. Or fixing the Deployment YAML but forgetting the image still does not exist.

Practical flow: kubectl describe pod and read the Events line - it literally says manifest unknown, unauthorized, or no such host. That word points straight at name, secret, or network.

Rule to remember: ImagePullBackOff is a fetch problem - check name, tag, secret, network.
