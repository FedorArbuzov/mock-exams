How do we agree what 'the service is healthy' means?

In simple terms, sLO is an internal reliability target; SLA is an external customer commitment.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: SLO is an internal reliability target; SLA is an external customer commitment. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Trust a few signals mapped to user pain: errors, latency, saturation, restarts, Ready replicas.

Alert only on pages you will actually act on — noise trains teams to ignore everything.
