How do I avoid hand-creating a ConfigMap on every file change?

In simple terms, generators build objects from files and help keep references updated.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: Generators build objects from files and help keep references updated. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Objects encode intent. A Deployment keeps N healthy copies. A Job runs to completion. Labels are how other objects find your Pods.

Before more YAML, ask what lifecycle you want: long-running, one-shot, per-node, or identity-stable — then choose the matching object.

Beginner trap: creating naked Pods and turning ops into babysitting.
