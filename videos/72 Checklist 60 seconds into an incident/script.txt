The service is down - which commands do you run in the first 60 seconds?

Follow a fixed four-step sweep so you never freeze under pressure.

Step one: kubectl get pods, add dash o wide. Are Pods Running and Ready, or do you see CrashLoop, Pending, or ImagePull problems? This classifies the failure instantly. Step two: kubectl describe pod on the bad one and read the Events - they name the real reason: FailedScheduling, ImagePullBackOff, Unhealthy probe. Step three: kubectl logs, and kubectl logs dash dash previous if it is crash-looping, to see the application's own error. Step four: check the wiring - kubectl get endpoints for the Service, and kubectl rollout status for the Deployment to confirm the latest deploy actually finished.

What beginners get wrong: jumping straight to logs when the Pod never started, or restarting things blindly before reading a single Event.

Practical tip: the order matters - status classifies, describe explains, logs confirm, endpoints and rollout catch the wiring. Say it out loud as you go.

Rule to remember: get, describe, logs, endpoints - four commands before you touch anything.
