How do I check a permission before trying the action?

In simple terms, the command quickly validates RBAC access before you run the operation.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: The command quickly validates RBAC access before you run the operation. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Keep a tiny trusted command set. Short commands survive stress and stay readable when shown large on a phone screen.

Know what each command proves: get is inventory, describe is why, logs are the app voice, rollout is release health.

Beginner trap: pasting flag encyclopedias you cannot explain.
