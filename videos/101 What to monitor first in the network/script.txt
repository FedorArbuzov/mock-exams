Which network metrics actually matter early on?

In simple terms, errors, latency, endpoint availability, and traffic saturation.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: Errors, latency, endpoint availability, and traffic saturation. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

This is how you move faster under pressure: name the layer, pick one proof, then change only what that proof supports.

If you skip the model, you will thrash — restarting things, editing YAML at random, and calling it debugging.

Beginner trap: knowing the buzzword while having no next check.

If someone asks tomorrow what this changes, answer with the rule: errors, latency, endpoint availability, and traffic saturation.
