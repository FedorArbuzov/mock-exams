How do I avoid shipping dev into prod?

In simple terms, always verify current context/namespace before applying changes.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: Always verify current context/namespace before applying changes. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Separate the brain from the factory floor. Control-plane failure and worker failure can look identical to users and are completely different to operators.

Ask four questions: can I reach the API, can Pods schedule, is the node Ready, and is the workload itself healthy?

Beginner trap: saying 'Kubernetes is down' without naming a layer.

For the video, huge text before apply: kubectl config current-context.

If someone asks tomorrow what this changes, answer with the rule: always verify current context/namespace before applying changes.
