Where does a request actually go after you open the site?

In simple terms, traffic passes through routing layers until it hits a backend Pod.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: Traffic passes through routing layers until it hits a backend Pod. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Most traffic outages are mismatches: selector, port, namespace, readiness, or Ingress rules with no controller.

Trace one request hop by hop — client, edge, Service, endpoints, Ready Pod — and stop at the first broken hop.

Beginner trap: blaming app code before confirming endpoints exist.

If someone asks tomorrow what this changes, answer with the rule: traffic passes through routing layers until it hits a backend Pod.
