What is an IDP and why do teams need it?

In simple terms, a platform provides paved paths (deploy/logs/secrets) so teams don't rebuild k8s from scratch.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: A platform provides paved paths (deploy/logs/secrets) so teams don't rebuild k8s from scratch. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

This is how you move faster under pressure: name the layer, pick one proof, then change only what that proof supports.
