What should be on one on-call screen?

In simple terms, ready replicas, restarts, latency/errors, rollout status.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: Ready replicas, restarts, latency/errors, rollout status. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Objects encode intent. A Deployment keeps N healthy copies. A Job runs to completion. Labels are how other objects find your Pods.

Before more YAML, ask what lifecycle you want: long-running, one-shot, per-node, or identity-stable — then choose the matching object.

Beginner trap: creating naked Pods and turning ops into babysitting.

For the video, huge text: kubectl rollout status deploy/web.
