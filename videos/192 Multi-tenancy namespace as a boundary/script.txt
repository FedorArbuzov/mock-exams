How do I share a cluster across teams at the start?

In simple terms, namespace + RBAC + quotas is the minimum shared-use model.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: Namespace + RBAC + quotas is the minimum shared-use model. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Objects encode intent. A Deployment keeps N healthy copies. A Job runs to completion. Labels are how other objects find your Pods.

Before more YAML, ask what lifecycle you want: long-running, one-shot, per-node, or identity-stable — then choose the matching object.

Beginner trap: creating naked Pods and turning ops into babysitting.
