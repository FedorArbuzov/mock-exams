How do I keep data if a Pod restarts?

In simple terms, a PV provides a volume; a PVC requests it for a workload.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: A PV provides a volume; a PVC requests it for a workload. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Pods can be replaced. Data usually cannot. Know where bytes live, how volumes are provisioned, and what delete does.

A backup never restored is a story, not a plan — rehearse restore in a throwaway namespace.

Beginner trap: treating persistent claims like temporary EmptyDir.

If someone asks tomorrow what this changes, answer with the rule: a PV provides a volume; a PVC requests it for a workload.
