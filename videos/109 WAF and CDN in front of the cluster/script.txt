Does protection start in Kubernetes or earlier?

In simple terms, cDN/WAF cut external threats; NetworkPolicy and RBAC protect inside the cluster.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: CDN/WAF cut external threats; NetworkPolicy and RBAC protect inside the cluster. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Separate the brain from the factory floor. Control-plane failure and worker failure can look identical to users and are completely different to operators.

Ask four questions: can I reach the API, can Pods schedule, is the node Ready, and is the workload itself healthy?

Beginner trap: saying 'Kubernetes is down' without naming a layer.
