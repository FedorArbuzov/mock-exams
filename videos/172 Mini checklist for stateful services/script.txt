What to check before putting stateful in prod?

In simple terms, storage, backup/restore, resilience, and latency/error monitoring.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: Storage, backup/restore, resilience, and latency/error monitoring. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Most traffic outages are mismatches: selector, port, namespace, readiness, or Ingress rules with no controller.

Trace one request hop by hop — client, edge, Service, endpoints, Ready Pod — and stop at the first broken hop.

Beginner trap: blaming app code before confirming endpoints exist.

If someone asks tomorrow what this changes, answer with the rule: storage, backup/restore, resilience, and latency/error monitoring.
