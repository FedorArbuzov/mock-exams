Why is a local node folder a bad prod idea?

In simple terms, hostPath ties data to one node and hurts portability.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: HostPath ties data to one node and hurts portability. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Pods can be replaced. Data usually cannot. Know where bytes live, how volumes are provisioned, and what delete does.

A backup never restored is a story, not a plan — rehearse restore in a throwaway namespace.

Beginner trap: treating persistent claims like temporary EmptyDir.
