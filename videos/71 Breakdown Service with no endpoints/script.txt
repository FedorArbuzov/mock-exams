Your Service exists but its endpoints are empty - why?

An empty endpoints list means the Service matched zero ready Pods, so traffic has nowhere to go. Three causes cover almost every case.

First, a selector mismatch. The Service's selector labels must exactly match the Pod's labels. One typo - app equals api versus app equals API - and the Service finds nothing. Second, Pods are not Ready. Even matching Pods only become endpoints when their readiness probe passes, so a failing probe leaves endpoints empty. Third, the wrong namespace. A Service only selects Pods in its own namespace; put them apart and they never connect.

What beginners get wrong: debugging DNS or Ingress when the real gap is here at the Service-to-Pod link. Or assuming a running Pod is automatically an endpoint, forgetting readiness.

Practical flow: kubectl get endpoints your-svc - if empty, the Service selects nothing. Compare kubectl get svc dash o wide selector labels with kubectl get pods dash dash show-labels. Check the pods are Ready and in the same namespace.

Rule to remember: no endpoints means selector, readiness, or namespace - check those three first.
