When list variables one by one vs load them all?

In simple terms, `env` is selective; `envFrom` loads an entire ConfigMap/Secret as env vars.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: `env` is selective; `envFrom` loads an entire ConfigMap/Secret as env vars. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

These fields are runtime levers for scheduling, restarts, eviction, and placement — not decorative YAML.

Change one field, watch Ready state and Events, and the concept becomes cause and effect.

Beginner trap: copying a full internet snippet without knowing the failure mode it prevents.
