Which RBAC mistakes most often break security?

In simple terms, broad wildcard permissions and shared accounts without isolation.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: Broad wildcard permissions and shared accounts without isolation. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Security is blast-radius math: who can read this, who can change this, and what a stolen identity can do next.

Prefer dedicated ServiceAccounts, narrow Roles, and explicit NetworkPolicy over convenient shared defaults.

Beginner trap: cluster-admin culture and default ServiceAccount everywhere.

If someone asks tomorrow what this changes, answer with the rule: broad wildcard permissions and shared accounts without isolation.
