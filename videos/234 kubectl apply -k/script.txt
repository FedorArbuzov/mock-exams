How do I apply kustomize without extra tools?

In simple terms, `apply -k` renders and applies a kustomization directory in one command.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: `apply -k` renders and applies a kustomization directory in one command. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Keep a tiny trusted command set. Short commands survive stress and stay readable when shown large on a phone screen.

Know what each command proves: get is inventory, describe is why, logs are the app voice, rollout is release health.

Beginner trap: pasting flag encyclopedias you cannot explain.

For the video, huge text: kubectl apply -k overlays/prod.
