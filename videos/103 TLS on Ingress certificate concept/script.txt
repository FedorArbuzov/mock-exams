How do I enable HTTPS on inbound traffic?

In simple terms, ingress references a TLS Secret with cert and key for the host.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: Ingress references a TLS Secret with cert and key for the host. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Most traffic outages are mismatches: selector, port, namespace, readiness, or Ingress rules with no controller.

Trace one request hop by hop — client, edge, Service, endpoints, Ready Pod — and stop at the first broken hop.

Beginner trap: blaming app code before confirming endpoints exist.
