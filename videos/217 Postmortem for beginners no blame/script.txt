How do we review incidents so the team grows?

In simple terms, focus on system and process causes, not finding someone to blame.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: Focus on system and process causes, not finding someone to blame. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Trust a few signals mapped to user pain: errors, latency, saturation, restarts, Ready replicas.

Alert only on pages you will actually act on — noise trains teams to ignore everything.

Beginner trap: forty dashboards and no trusted symptom.

If someone asks tomorrow what this changes, answer with the rule: focus on system and process causes, not finding someone to blame.
