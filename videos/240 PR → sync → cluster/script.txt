What does a GitOps workday look like?

In simple terms, change via Pull Request, merge, automatic sync, then health checks.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: Change via Pull Request, merge, automatic sync, then health checks. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Separate the brain from the factory floor. Control-plane failure and worker failure can look identical to users and are completely different to operators.

Ask four questions: can I reach the API, can Pods schedule, is the node Ready, and is the workload itself healthy?

Beginner trap: saying 'Kubernetes is down' without naming a layer.

If someone asks tomorrow what this changes, answer with the rule: change via Pull Request, merge, automatic sync, then health checks.
