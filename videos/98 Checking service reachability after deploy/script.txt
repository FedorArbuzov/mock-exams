How do I quickly know the service is really alive?

In simple terms, check endpoints, Pod readiness, and a real application response.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: Check endpoints, Pod readiness, and a real application response. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Most traffic outages are mismatches: selector, port, namespace, readiness, or Ingress rules with no controller.

Trace one request hop by hop — client, edge, Service, endpoints, Ready Pod — and stop at the first broken hop.

Beginner trap: blaming app code before confirming endpoints exist.

For the video, a tiny readinessProbe block — path and port only, huge font.
