A favorite interviewer trick question?

In simple terms, new Pods on that node won't be managed correctly; already running containers may keep going, but state control breaks.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: New Pods on that node won't be managed correctly; already running containers may keep going, but state control breaks. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Separate the brain from the factory floor. Control-plane failure and worker failure can look identical to users and are completely different to operators.

Ask four questions: can I reach the API, can Pods schedule, is the node Ready, and is the workload itself healthy?

Beginner trap: saying 'Kubernetes is down' without naming a layer.
