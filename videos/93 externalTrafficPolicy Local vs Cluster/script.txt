In simple terms, local helps preserve client IP; Cluster balances more broadly across nodes.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: Local helps preserve client IP; Cluster balances more broadly across nodes. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Separate the brain from the factory floor. Control-plane failure and worker failure can look identical to users and are completely different to operators.

Ask four questions: can I reach the API, can Pods schedule, is the node Ready, and is the workload itself healthy?
