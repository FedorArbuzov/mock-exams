I have a backup — how do I know restore actually works?

In simple terms, regularly test restore in a separate environment.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: Regularly test restore in a separate environment. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Pods can be replaced. Data usually cannot. Know where bytes live, how volumes are provisioned, and what delete does.

A backup never restored is a story, not a plan — rehearse restore in a throwaway namespace.

Beginner trap: treating persistent claims like temporary EmptyDir.
