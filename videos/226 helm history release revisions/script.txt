How do I see which versions were already rolled out?

In simple terms, history lists revisions and statuses for analysis and rollback.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: History lists revisions and statuses for analysis and rollback. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Keep a tiny trusted command set. Short commands survive stress and stay readable when shown large on a phone screen.

Know what each command proves: get is inventory, describe is why, logs are the app voice, rollout is release health.

Beginner trap: pasting flag encyclopedias you cannot explain.
