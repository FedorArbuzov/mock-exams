How do you avoid sleep loops in CI scripts?

kubectl wait. It blocks until a resource reaches a condition you name, then exits success or times out. Instead of sleep sixty and hope, you write kubectl wait dash dash for equals condition equals Available deployment slash my-app dash dash timeout equals 120s.

That is the right pattern for pipelines. Wait for a Job to complete, a Pod to be Ready, or a rollout to finish before the next step runs tests. Timeouts fail the job clearly when the cluster never gets healthy.

What beginners get wrong: sleeping fixed amounts that are too short on slow clusters and too long on fast ones. Or waiting on the wrong condition - Available versus Ready versus delete. Also forgetting dash dash for equals delete when you need an object gone before recreate.

Practical tip: pair wait with kubectl rollout status for Deployments, and always set an explicit timeout so CI cannot hang forever.

Rule to remember: wait on conditions, do not sleep and pray.
