How do I automatically ban dangerous manifests?

In simple terms, policies-as-code check manifests in CI and/or at the API gate.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: Policies-as-code check manifests in CI and/or at the API gate. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Security is blast-radius math: who can read this, who can change this, and what a stolen identity can do next.

Prefer dedicated ServiceAccounts, narrow Roles, and explicit NetworkPolicy over convenient shared defaults.

Beginner trap: cluster-admin culture and default ServiceAccount everywhere.
