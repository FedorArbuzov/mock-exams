Pod suddenly vanished — bug or cluster policy?

In simple terms, eviction happens under node memory/disk pressure to protect cluster stability.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: Eviction happens under node memory/disk pressure to protect cluster stability. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

When it breaks, walk a short path: Pod status, Events, logs --previous, then the owner like a Deployment or Job. Random restarts are not a strategy.

Your job is to name the layer: image pull, config, crash, scheduling, or a controller recreating bad state on purpose.

Beginner trap: deleting Pods in a loop and calling it troubleshooting.

If someone asks tomorrow what this changes, answer with the rule: eviction happens under node memory/disk pressure to protect cluster stability.
