Where should a Kubernetes app write logs?

In simple terms, write to stdout/stderr — the logging agent/platform will collect them.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: Write to stdout/stderr — the logging agent/platform will collect them. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Trust a few signals mapped to user pain: errors, latency, saturation, restarts, Ready replicas.

Alert only on pages you will actually act on — noise trains teams to ignore everything.

Beginner trap: forty dashboards and no trusted symptom.

If someone asks tomorrow what this changes, answer with the rule: write to stdout/stderr — the logging agent/platform will collect them.
