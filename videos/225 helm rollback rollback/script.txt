How do I quickly return to a working version after a bad upgrade?

In simple terms, rollback restores a previous release revision.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: Rollback restores a previous release revision. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Keep a tiny trusted command set. Short commands survive stress and stay readable when shown large on a phone screen.

Know what each command proves: get is inventory, describe is why, logs are the app voice, rollout is release health.

Beginner trap: pasting flag encyclopedias you cannot explain.

For the video, huge text: helm rollback web 1.
