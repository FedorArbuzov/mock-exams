Is a base64 Secret actually secure?

In simple terms, base64 is encoding; real security comes from access control and storage encryption.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: Base64 is encoding; real security comes from access control and storage encryption. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Objects encode intent. A Deployment keeps N healthy copies. A Job runs to completion. Labels are how other objects find your Pods.

Before more YAML, ask what lifecycle you want: long-running, one-shot, per-node, or identity-stable — then choose the matching object.
