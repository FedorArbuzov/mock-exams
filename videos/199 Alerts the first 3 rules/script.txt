Which alerts should I start with so I don't drown?

In simple terms, high error rate, readiness failures, and CPU/memory saturation.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: High error rate, readiness failures, and CPU/memory saturation. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Trust a few signals mapped to user pain: errors, latency, saturation, restarts, Ready replicas.

Alert only on pages you will actually act on — noise trains teams to ignore everything.

Beginner trap: forty dashboards and no trusted symptom.

For the video, a tiny readinessProbe block — path and port only, huge font.

If someone asks tomorrow what this changes, answer with the rule: high error rate, readiness failures, and CPU/memory saturation.
