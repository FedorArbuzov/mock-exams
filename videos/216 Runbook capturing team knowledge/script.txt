How do we stop solving the same incident from scratch?

In simple terms, a runbook records diagnosis and recovery steps for repeatable situations.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: A runbook records diagnosis and recovery steps for repeatable situations. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Trust a few signals mapped to user pain: errors, latency, saturation, restarts, Ready replicas.

Alert only on pages you will actually act on — noise trains teams to ignore everything.
