How do I rotate tokens without service downtime?

In simple terms, rotation means updating the Secret, recreating Pods, and verifying readiness.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: Rotation means updating the Secret, recreating Pods, and verifying readiness. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Objects encode intent. A Deployment keeps N healthy copies. A Job runs to completion. Labels are how other objects find your Pods.

Before more YAML, ask what lifecycle you want: long-running, one-shot, per-node, or identity-stable — then choose the matching object.
