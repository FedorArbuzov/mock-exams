Helm templates or Kustomize overlays?

In simple terms, kustomize shines at template-free patches; Helm shines at packaging and release parameterization.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: Kustomize shines at template-free patches; Helm shines at packaging and release parameterization. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Keep a tiny trusted command set. Short commands survive stress and stay readable when shown large on a phone screen.

Know what each command proves: get is inventory, describe is why, logs are the app voice, rollout is release health.

Beginner trap: pasting flag encyclopedias you cannot explain.

If someone asks tomorrow what this changes, answer with the rule: kustomize shines at template-free patches; Helm shines at packaging and release parameterization.
