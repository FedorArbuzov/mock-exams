Why is the Pod stuck in Pending?

In simple terms, usually missing resources, taint constraints, or PVC problems.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: Usually missing resources, taint constraints, or PVC problems. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

When it breaks, walk a short path: Pod status, Events, logs --previous, then the owner like a Deployment or Job. Random restarts are not a strategy.

Your job is to name the layer: image pull, config, crash, scheduling, or a controller recreating bad state on purpose.

Beginner trap: deleting Pods in a loop and calling it troubleshooting.

If someone asks tomorrow what this changes, answer with the rule: usually missing resources, taint constraints, or PVC problems.
