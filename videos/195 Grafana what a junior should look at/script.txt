Which dashboard should I open first?

In simple terms, start with the service's RED/Golden Signals and Pod/node utilization.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: Start with the service's RED/Golden Signals and Pod/node utilization. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Trust a few signals mapped to user pain: errors, latency, saturation, restarts, Ready replicas.

Alert only on pages you will actually act on — noise trains teams to ignore everything.
