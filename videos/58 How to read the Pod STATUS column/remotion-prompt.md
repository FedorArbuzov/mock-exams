Scene 1: What do Running, Pending, and CrashLoopBackOff mean?
Scene 2: The STATUS column is a short summary of a Pod's phase and current condition - the cluster's one-word answer to how are you. Running means containers are up. Pending means not scheduled or not ready. CrashLoopBackOff means the container keeps dying and Kubernetes is backing off.
Scene 3: But STATUS is only a headline, not the story. It tells you something is wrong, not why. The reason lives in describe and Events, and the app-level detail lives in logs.
Scene 4: What beginners get wrong: treating STATUS as a full diagnosis and refreshing get pods hoping it flips. Or panicking at ContainerCreating, which is often a normal in-progress state. Flow: STATUS classifies, describe explains, logs confirm.
Scene 5: Rule to remember: STATUS is the headline - describe and logs are the article.
