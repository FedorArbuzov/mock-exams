Which port goes where — endless confusion?

In simple terms, containerPort is in the container; port is on the Service; targetPort is where traffic hits the Pod.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: containerPort is in the container; port is on the Service; targetPort is where traffic hits the Pod. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Most traffic outages are mismatches: selector, port, namespace, readiness, or Ingress rules with no controller.

Trace one request hop by hop — client, edge, Service, endpoints, Ready Pod — and stop at the first broken hop.

Beginner trap: blaming app code before confirming endpoints exist.
