The image pulled fine, but the Pod still won't start a container - why?

That is often CreateContainerConfigError. The kubelet has the image but cannot build a valid container configuration, so it never launches. The cause is almost always a missing or wrong reference in your Pod spec.

The usual suspect: you reference a ConfigMap or Secret that does not exist, or a specific key inside it that is not there. A valueFrom pointing at configMapKeyRef or secretKeyRef with a typo triggers this instantly. Same for mounting a Secret volume that was never created in that namespace.

What beginners get wrong: confusing this with ImagePullBackOff, since both stop the container from starting. But here the image is fine - the config is broken. Also creating the ConfigMap in the wrong namespace, so the Pod cannot see it.

Practical flow: kubectl describe pod and read Events - it names the missing ConfigMap, Secret, or key. Then kubectl get configmap and secret in the same namespace to confirm they exist.

Rule to remember: CreateContainerConfigError means a missing ConfigMap, Secret, or key - not the image.
