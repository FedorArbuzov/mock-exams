Why is `cluster-admin` almost always a bad idea?

In simple terms, least privilege reduces the blast radius of mistakes and compromise.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: Least privilege reduces the blast radius of mistakes and compromise. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Security is blast-radius math: who can read this, who can change this, and what a stolen identity can do next.

Prefer dedicated ServiceAccounts, narrow Roles, and explicit NetworkPolicy over convenient shared defaults.

Beginner trap: cluster-admin culture and default ServiceAccount everywhere.

If someone asks tomorrow what this changes, answer with the rule: least privilege reduces the blast radius of mistakes and compromise.
