How do I quickly judge how dangerous a Pod is?

In simple terms, pSS defines levels of restrictions on container and host privileges.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: PSS defines levels of restrictions on container and host privileges. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Security is blast-radius math: who can read this, who can change this, and what a stolen identity can do next.

Prefer dedicated ServiceAccounts, narrow Roles, and explicit NetworkPolicy over convenient shared defaults.

Beginner trap: cluster-admin culture and default ServiceAccount everywhere.
