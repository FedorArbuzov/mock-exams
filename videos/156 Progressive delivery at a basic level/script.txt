How do I roll out carefully instead of to everyone at once?

In simple terms, gradually increase traffic/replica share after quality signals look good.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: Gradually increase traffic/replica share after quality signals look good. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Delivery quality is a bored repeatable path: pinned image, Git as source of truth, sync, smoke, rollback.

If humans must remember secret dashboard clicks, drift is already invited.

Beginner trap: kubectl edit in prod with no path back to Git.
