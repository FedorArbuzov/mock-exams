What happens to data after deleting a PVC?

In simple terms, reclaim policy decides whether to delete the volume or keep it for recovery.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: Reclaim policy decides whether to delete the volume or keep it for recovery. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Pods can be replaced. Data usually cannot. Know where bytes live, how volumes are provisioned, and what delete does.

A backup never restored is a story, not a plan — rehearse restore in a throwaway namespace.

Beginner trap: treating persistent claims like temporary EmptyDir.
