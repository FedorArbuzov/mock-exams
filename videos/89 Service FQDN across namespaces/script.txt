When is a short service name not enough?

In simple terms, for explicit cross-namespace addressing, use the full service DNS name.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: For explicit cross-namespace addressing, use the full service DNS name. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Objects encode intent. A Deployment keeps N healthy copies. A Job runs to completion. Labels are how other objects find your Pods.

Before more YAML, ask what lifecycle you want: long-running, one-shot, per-node, or identity-stable — then choose the matching object.
