Why can I call `my-service` without an IP?

In simple terms, built-in DNS resolves service names to in-cluster addresses.

Concretely, treat that as a testable rule: Built-in DNS resolves service names to in-cluster addresses. When reality disagrees, trust cluster signals over the story in your head.

Most traffic outages are mismatches: selector, port, namespace, readiness, or Ingress rules with no controller.

Trace one request hop by hop — client, edge, Service, endpoints, Ready Pod — and stop at the first broken hop.

Beginner trap: blaming app code before confirming endpoints exist.
