How do I detach from the backing array?

Use copy(dst, src) into a buffer you own, or slices.Clone in modern Go. That breaks sharing so later appends on one slice do not stomp another.

copy returns how many elements copied — useful with partial buffers.

When passing subslices to long-lived goroutines, clone first unless you designed the sharing intentionally.

Detach when lifetime or mutation independence matters.
