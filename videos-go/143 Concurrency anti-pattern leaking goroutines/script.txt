Why does memory grow forever?

Leaked goroutines. Every goroutine blocked forever on a channel or lock holds stack and references, preventing GC of connected objects.

Common causes: forgotten close, missing ctx cancel, WaitGroup mismatch, sending without receivers.

Profile goroutines with pprof goroutine endpoint when heap climbs but traffic is flat.

Leak fix: every go statement needs an exit — cancel, close, timeout, or completion.
