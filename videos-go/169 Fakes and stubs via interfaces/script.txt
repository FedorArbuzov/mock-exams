Do I need a mocking framework?

Often no. Define a small interface, write a fake struct in _test.go with canned responses. Hand-written fakes are readable and compile-time checked.

Mock generators help huge interfaces — but huge interfaces are already a smell.

FakeDB with map storage beats code-generated mocks for most unit tests.
