Go modules let you swap a dependency for a local checkout while you work. How do I test a local fork?

Add a replace directive in your go.mod. It points your module's import path to a local folder instead of the cached remote version.

Replace is for local development and emergency patches — not a long-term publishing strategy. Comment why it's there, so it doesn't ship to production by accident.

Remove the replace line before release, or make sure your CI uses the real module version.

Dev trick: point replace at your own fork while you fix a bug upstream.
