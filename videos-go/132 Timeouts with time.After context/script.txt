How do I stop waiting forever?

Combine select with time.After for deadlines or, better, with context.Context deadlines. ctx, cancel := context.WithTimeout(parent, 5*time.Second) then select on work versus ctx.Done().

Bare time.After in tight loops can leak timers — context is preferred for request-scoped timeouts.

Always defer cancel() after WithTimeout or WithCancel.

Timeout habit: context.WithTimeout plus select — not sleep guesses.
