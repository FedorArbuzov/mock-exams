Should I disallow mystery JSON?

For strict APIs, yes. json.NewDecoder(r.Body).DisallowUnknownFields() rejects requests with unexpected keys — catches typos and version skew early.

Loose decoding is fine for exploratory tools. Public APIs benefit from strictness.

Combine with validation after decode — types alone do not enforce business rules.
