Mutex for a counter feels heavy

For simple counters and flags, sync/atomic offers lock-free operations: atomic.AddInt64, atomic.LoadInt64. Faster for hot stats paths when you do not need complex invariants.

Atomics do not replace mutexes for multi-field updates that must stay consistent together.

Use atomics for metrics; mutex for structured state.
