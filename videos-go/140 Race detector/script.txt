How do I find data races?

Run go test -race ./... or go run -race on programs with concurrency. The race detector instruments memory access and reports unsynchronized reads and writes.

It slows execution — use in CI and local tests, not always in prod.

Fix races with mutexes, channels, or confining data to one goroutine. "It works on my laptop" is not synchronization.

Race habit: -race on every PR that touches goroutines or shared state.
