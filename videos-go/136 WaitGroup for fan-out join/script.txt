How do I wait for N workers?

sync.WaitGroup: wg.Add(n) before starting work, defer wg.Done() in each goroutine, wg.Wait() to block until all finish.

WaitGroup joins success paths — it does not collect errors. Pair with err channel or errgroup when failures matter.

Add before go — race if you Add after Wait starts.

Join pattern: Add, go worker with Done, Wait — error handling needs more.
