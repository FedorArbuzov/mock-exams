What does range give me?

Over a slice or array: index and value. Over a map: key and value. Over a channel: values until closed. Over a string: index and rune.

Map iteration order is randomized on purpose — do not depend on key order. If you need sorted keys, collect keys and sort them.

For slices, value is a copy unless you use index to mutate slice[i].

Range rule: map order is random; channel range until close; string range gives runes.
