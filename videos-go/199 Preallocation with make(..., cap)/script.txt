Why is append slow in a loop?

Repeated append without capacity grows the backing array multiple times — copy on each growth. Preallocate: s := make([]T, 0, expectedLen).

Benchmark with -benchmem to see allocations drop after pre-sizing.

Know expected size from metrics or config — do not guess huge capacities blindly.

Slice perf: make with cap before append loop — cut allocations.
