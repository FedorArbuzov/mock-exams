How do I construct a struct clearly?

Use keyed struct literals: User{Name: "Ann", Age: 30}. Keys resist breakage when fields are reordered or added.

Positional literals User{"Ann", 30} break silently when someone inserts a new field.

For small internal structs with obvious order, positional is fine. For APIs and configs, always key your fields.

Literal rule: keyed fields for anything that leaves the package or might evolve.
