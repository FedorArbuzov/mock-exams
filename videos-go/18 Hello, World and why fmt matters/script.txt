Why not just print somehow?

fmt is the standard formatting and printing package. fmt.Println for simple output, fmt.Printf when you need verbs like %s, %d, and %q.

Learning fmt early matters because logs, errors, and debug output all reuse the same verbs. You will live in fmt.Sprintf when building error messages too.

Hello World is not trivia — it proves your module, toolchain, and editor all agree.

On screen try: fmt.Printf("user=%s id=%d\n", name, id) — verbs beat string concatenation for clarity.
