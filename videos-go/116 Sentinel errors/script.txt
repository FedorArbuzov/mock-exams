When do I export var ErrX = errors.New(...)?

When callers must branch on an expected condition — not found, conflict, canceled. Export the sentinel, document when it returns, and keep the message stable.

Do not export every error. Most failures stay private strings wrapped upward.

Sentinels are API contracts. Changing them breaks callers who errors.Is on them.

Sentinel rule: exported when callers need to recognize it; private otherwise.
