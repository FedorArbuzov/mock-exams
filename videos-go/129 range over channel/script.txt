How do I consume until done?

for v := range ch { process(v) } loops until the channel is closed. Closing signals no more values — receivers drain and exit.

Only the sender should close — never close from the receiver. Closing an already closed channel panics.

Range plus close is the standard worker shutdown pattern.

Consumer pattern: range until close, close from producer side only.
