My function is a Christmas tree of braces

Flatten it. Replace else branches with early returns. Extract nested blocks into named helper functions with clear names.

Each level of indentation is a cognitive tax. Future readers — including you at 3 a.m. — will miss a branch.

Go does not reward clever nesting. It rewards functions small enough to hold in your head.

Refactor trigger: more than two levels of else — extract or return early.
