Does switch fall through like C?

No — not by default. Each case breaks automatically. You need an explicit fallthrough keyword to drop into the next case, and that is rare.

That prevents the classic C bug where you forget break and execute three cases accidentally.

Switch works on values, types with type switch, and even no condition like a readable if-else chain.

Go switch is safer by default. Use it instead of long if-else ladders when comparing one expression.
