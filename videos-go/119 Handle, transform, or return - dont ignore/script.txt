Is _ = err ever OK?

Almost never. Ignoring errors hides data loss, auth bypasses, and partial writes. At minimum log at the boundary with reason.

If you truly must ignore — rare cleanup cases — comment why loudly.

go vet and linters flag many ignored errors. Treat err != nil as non-optional unless documented.

Discipline: handle, wrap and return, or log — never silent underscore.
