What does idiomatic Go even mean?

It means code a new teammate can read without decoding your personal framework. Clear names, small functions, explicit error handling, and composition over inheritance tricks.

The Go community repeats: clear is better than clever. That shows up in reviews: rejected PRs often are not wrong — they are just too magical.

Clever one-liners age badly. Boring code survives job changes, on-call nights, and junior contributors.

Daily habit: name things for what they do, return errors instead of hiding them, and delete abstractions nobody asked for.
