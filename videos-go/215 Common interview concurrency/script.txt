Goroutines vs threads?

Goroutines are cheap, multiplexed by the Go runtime onto fewer OS threads. Start thousands for IO-bound work with proper lifecycle control.

Communicate with channels or protect shared state with mutex. Use context for cancelation.

Always mention go test -race and goroutine leak risks in interviews.

Interview answer: cheap goroutines, channels or mutex, context cancel, race detector.
