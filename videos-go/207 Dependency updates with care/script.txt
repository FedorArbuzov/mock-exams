Should I always go get -u everything?

No. Update dependencies deliberately — read changelogs, run full tests, update one major dependency at a time when possible.

Blind updates break builds on Friday and hide which library caused the regression.

Use dependabot or renovate with CI — not manual -u on everything monthly panic.
