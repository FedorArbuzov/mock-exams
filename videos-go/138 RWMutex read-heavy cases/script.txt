All locks are the same?

sync.RWMutex allows many concurrent readers or one writer. Useful when reads dominate and writes are rare — caches, config snapshots.

It is slower than Mutex for write-heavy workloads. Benchmark before assuming RWMutex helps.

Always pair with defer mu.Unlock() or defer mu.RUnlock() immediately after lock.
