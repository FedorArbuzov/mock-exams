What's the concurrency review list?

Who closes channels? Is context propagated and honored? Are maps protected or single-goroutine? Did you run the race detector? Is parallelism bounded?

Also check: defers in loops, select without default spinning CPU, and errgroup versus naked WaitGroup for errors.
