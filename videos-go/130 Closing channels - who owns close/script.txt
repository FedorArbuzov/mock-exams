Who is allowed to close?

The sender closes to signal no more values. Receivers read until drained. Never close a channel you do not own. With multiple senders, use sync.Once or a dedicated coordinator — or avoid close and use context cancel instead.

close(ch) on a closed channel panics. Sending on a closed channel panics.

When in doubt, cancel context and let receivers exit — simpler than channel close choreography.
