Why are my tests painful?

Probably because business logic is tangled with database, HTTP, and filesystem calls. Pure functions that take data and return data are trivial to table-test.

Push IO to the edges. Keep the center deterministic. That is the same lesson as hexagonal architecture, just smaller.

If you need mocks everywhere, your functions may be doing too much.

Testability rule: separate decide from do — logic without IO in the middle.
