What should /health return?

Split liveness and readiness. Liveness: process up — simple 200. Readiness: can serve traffic — DB connected, migrations done, dependencies OK.

Do not put deep checks on liveness — flapping restarts kill pods. Readiness can fail when dependencies are down.

Kubernetes uses both probes differently — conflating them causes outages.

Health endpoints: /healthz live, /readyz ready — different checks.
