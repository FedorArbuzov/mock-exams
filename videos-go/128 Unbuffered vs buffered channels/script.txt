Why does my send block forever?

Unbuffered channels block until another goroutine receives. If nobody receives, you deadlock. Buffered channels block only when the buffer is full.

make(chan int) syncs handoff. make(chan int, 10) allows ten sends without a receiver waiting.

Pick buffer size with meaning — zero for handshake, small for backpressure, not random 1000.

Channel sizing: unbuffered for sync, buffered for bounded queues.
