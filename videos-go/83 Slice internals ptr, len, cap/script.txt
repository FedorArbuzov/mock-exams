Why did appending change another slice?

Slices are headers: pointer, length, capacity. Two slices can share one backing array. Append may overwrite slots another slice still sees until growth reallocates.

Always reassign: s = append(s, x). If you need independence, copy or clone.

Understanding ptr, len, cap explains half of junior slice bugs.

Mental model: slice is a window on an array — windows can overlap.
