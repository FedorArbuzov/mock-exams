Why does Go feel plain on purpose?

Because teams pay for readability over cleverness. Go deliberately omits many features other languages celebrate: no inheritance trees, limited operator overloading, opinionated formatting with gofmt.

That sounds limiting until you join a codebase with ten authors. Everyone writes similar-looking code. Reviews focus on logic, not dialect wars.

Boring does not mean weak. Go powers massive distributed systems. It just refuses to let every engineer invent a personal style.

Practical rule: if you reach for a clever abstraction, ask whether a plain function and a struct would be clearer to the next reader.
