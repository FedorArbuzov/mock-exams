How do I model domains without classes?

Describe behavior with small interfaces and compose structs. A PaymentProcessor interface, a Service struct holding dependencies, methods that orchestrate.

No inheritance tree — just data structures plus functions that operate on them through interfaces at boundaries.

Document behavior in interface names and method docs, not in deep class hierarchies.
