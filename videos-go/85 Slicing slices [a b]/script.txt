Does slicing copy data?

Usually no. s[low:high] creates a new slice header pointing at the same backing array. Mutating s[i] can affect siblings sharing that array.

Use copy or slices.Clone when you need an independent buffer. Sub-slices are views, not copies.

This matters when you pass sub-slices to goroutines or reuse a large buffer for small pieces.

Safety: slice for view, copy or Clone for ownership transfer.
