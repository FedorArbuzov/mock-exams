How do goroutines talk safely?

Channels pass values between goroutines. ch <- v sends, v := <-ch receives. They coordinate without shared mutable maps.

Use channels for ownership transfer and signals. Do not use them as a global variable substitute for everything.

Unbuffered channels synchronize — sender waits for receiver. Buffered channels decouple up to capacity.

Channel mindset: communicate ownership, do not broadcast mutable state blindly.
