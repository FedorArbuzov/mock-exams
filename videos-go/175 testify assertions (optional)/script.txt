Do I need assert libraries?

Standard testing is enough: if got != want { t.Fatalf(...) }. testify/assert reduces boilerplate if your team likes it — not required.

Assertions libraries do not replace thinking about what to test.

Pick one style per repo — mixed assert and manual checks confuse readers.
