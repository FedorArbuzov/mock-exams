Should I retry everything?

No. Retry transient network blips and 503s with backoff. Do not retry validation errors, 404s, or non-idempotent writes without deduplication.

Classify errors: retryable versus fatal. Pass that in typed errors or wrappers.

Retries without idempotency create duplicate charges and duplicate rows.

Retry rule: only idempotent operations, exponential backoff, cap attempts.
