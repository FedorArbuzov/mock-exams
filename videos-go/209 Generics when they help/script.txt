Should I generic everything?

No. Use generics when you have real duplication — min of two types, set operations, container helpers — not for aesthetics.

If a plain function or interface reads clearer, keep it. Generics add compile-time complexity readers must parse.

Good generic: removes three copy-pasted functions. Bad generic: saves five lines once.

Generics rule: remove duplication you actually have — not hypothetical duplication.
