Go has one official formatter for every file. Who decides tabs and braces?

gofmt does. Not you, not your reviewer, not a style debate in Slack. Run go fmt and commit the result.

That single decision saves thousands of hours across the ecosystem. Diffs show logic changes, not brace wars.

go fmt is also gofmt under the hood. Many editors run it on save — turn that on day one.

Team rule: never hand-format Go. If fmt changes your file, the file was wrong before.
