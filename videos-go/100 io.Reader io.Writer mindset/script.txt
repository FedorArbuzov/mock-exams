How does Go glue IO together?

With io.Reader and io.Writer — stream interfaces. Files, network connections, buffers, gzip wrappers, and strings all implement Reader or Writer.

io.Copy connects them without loading entire files into memory. That composability is core Go style.

Learn these two interfaces first — half the stdlib becomes familiar.

IO mindset: everything is a stream — Read in, Write out, Copy between.
