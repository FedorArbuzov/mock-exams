make vs literal?

make allocates slices, maps, and channels with optional length and capacity. literals build fixed content: []int{1,2,3} or map[string]int{"a":1}.

make([]int, 0, 100) pre-sizes a slice. make(map[string]int) gives a ready writable map. var m map[string]int is nil and not writable.

Pick make for dynamic empty collections, literals for known seed data.

Collection start: make when empty and growing, literal when you know the initial values.
