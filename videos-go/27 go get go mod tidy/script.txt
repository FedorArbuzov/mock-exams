Go manages dependencies through a couple of specific commands. How do dependencies not rot?

You add dependencies by importing them and running go mod tidy. Tidy adds what you use, removes what you do not, and updates your checksums.

go get bumps a specific dependency to a chosen version. Blind updates across everything is how teams break builds on Friday afternoons.

Read changelogs on upgrades. Run your tests after every dependency change.

Healthy habit: tidy before commit, never hand-edit your checksums file unless you know why.
