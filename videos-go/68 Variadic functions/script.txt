How does fmt.Println take many args?

Variadic functions use ...T in the parameter list. Inside the function, args is a slice of T. fmt.Println is the famous example.

You can spread a slice with fn(slice...) when calling a variadic function.

Use variadics for constructors and formatters — not for hiding required parameters.

Define sparingly: variadic options are great; variadic required data is confusing.
