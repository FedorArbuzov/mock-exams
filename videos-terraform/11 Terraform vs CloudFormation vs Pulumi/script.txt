Which infrastructure tool should you pick?

Terraform uses a declarative language and supports many providers through a consistent workflow. CloudFormation is deeply integrated with AWS and its native services. Pulumi lets teams use general-purpose languages, which can be attractive when infrastructure needs rich programming abstractions.

Choose based on your cloud scope, team skills, existing modules, governance needs, and operational support. A single-cloud AWS team may value CloudFormation integration, while a multi-platform team may prefer Terraform's ecosystem. Test small real workflows before standardizing across the organization.

Do not choose solely because a tool is fashionable or its syntax looks familiar. Migration cost, state management, provider maturity, and team troubleshooting experience matter long after the first demo.

Choose the tool your team can safely operate repeatedly.
