dash target is a scalpel, not a deployment strategy.

The -target option limits planning and applying to selected addresses and their dependencies. It can help recover from exceptional failures or isolate a specific repair. It intentionally bypasses Terraform's normal view of the complete desired infrastructure graph.

Use terraform plan -target only when you can explain why a full plan is unsafe or impossible. Review the warning and follow with a normal plan after the targeted change. The full plan confirms that unrelated dependencies remain consistent.

Regular targeted applies create partial updates and hide changes outside the selected path. Teams may think a deployment succeeded while an output, policy, or dependent resource remains outdated. Do not put targeting into routine CI deployment commands.
